Who I am
Engineer. Researcher.
Problem Solver.

I'm a results-driven engineer with over 12 years of experience spanning chemical process modeling, renewable energy, hydrogen production, and carbon capture & sequestration (CCS).

Currently, I work as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Argonne National Laboratory, where I lead technoeconomic analysis, life cycle assessments, and industrial process modeling for decarbonization projects across steel, chemical, and energy sectors.

My work bridges rigorous engineering fundamentals with real-world sustainability impact — from scaling adsorption systems for critical metal recovery to modeling green hydrogen production pathways using regional renewable energy sources.
